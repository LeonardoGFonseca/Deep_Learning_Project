# Session Summary: 10-04-2026 (Data Sovereignty & Scope Definition)

## 1. The Labeling Pipeline (The Orchestrator)
We have finalized the logical architecture for the data labeling pipeline, prioritizing reproducibility and existing optimized tools over manual C implementation.

### The DAG Logic:
- **Orchestration:** Bash script (`scripts/labeling_pipeline.sh`).
- **Basecalling:** `Dorado` (C++/CUDA) to translate POD5 to FASTQ.
- **Alignment:** `minimap2` (C) to map reads against the `blaNDM-1` reference.
- **Label Mapping:** Python script (`src/data/label_mapper.py`) to parse alignment scores and generate `master.csv`.
- **Thresholds:** Label 1 (Resistant) requires >90% Identity and >80% Gene Coverage.

## 2. Model Scope & Scientific Objective
We moved from vague "AMR Detection" to a specific, well-defined binary classification task.

- **Input:** 3,000-sample sliding windows, MAD-normalized to roughly $[-5, 5]$.
- **Target:** Specific Carbapenem-resistance genes (e.g., `blaNDM-1`).
- **Philosophy:** Species-agnostic training. The model learns the *motif* of the gene, not the *signature* of the bacteria.
- **Metrics:** Prioritizing AUC-ROC and F1-score due to the clinical high-stakes of False Negatives in AMR detection.

## 3. Engineering Decisions
- **Rejection of C for Pipelines:** Orchestration is handled by Bash/Python to minimize technical debt and maximize development speed.
- **EDA Requirement:** Mandatory visualization of raw vs. normalized signals before model training to verify the "Physics of the Pore."
